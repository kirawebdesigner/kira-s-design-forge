What gets automated

Start with the process, then choose the technology.

Useful automation begins with a clear understanding of triggers, decisions, ownership, exceptions, and the information people need at each step.

The result may be a focused integration, a backend workflow, an operator dashboard, a customer notification system, or a larger operational product. The goal is a reliable process, not automation for its own sake.

Questions

Before automating a workflow.

What information should I provide?

Describe the current manual process, who performs each step, the tools involved, common errors, and the outcome the automation should produce.

Can automation include human approval?

Yes. Reliable automation often keeps important decisions with an operator while handling data collection, validation, notifications, and routine transitions automatically.

How do we start?

Send the workflow, current tools, pain points, and required outcome to contact@kiraweb.pro.et.
